        How shows are labeled

        Each show gets its main period or movement, its medium and, where it matters, its culture or region. Shows by living artists that respond to an earlier tradition are tagged “in dialogue with” that tradition. They appear when you filter for it, ranked after shows of the period itself.

        “I’m in”

        Opens Google Calendar with the show’s name, address, hours and closing date filled in, on the next Saturday it’s open. Change the day and time, then save. Nothing is added until you do.

        Updated weekly

        Listings refresh every Sunday at 6 PM Eastern. Always confirm hours on the venue’s own page before you go.
